What this tool does

The Unit Converter helps convert everyday metric and imperial measurements, including values commonly used in the UK. It is useful for shopping, travel, recipes, parcel sizes, DIY work and study notes.

How to use it

  1. Open the Unit Converter and choose the measurement category.
  2. Enter the value you want to convert.
  3. Select the source and target units.
  4. Use the converted result for planning, checking or comparison.

Best use cases

  • Convert centimetres to inches, kilograms to pounds or miles to kilometres.
  • Check product dimensions before buying online.
  • Translate recipe quantities and travel measurements quickly.
  • Estimate parcel size, weight or distance for everyday planning.
