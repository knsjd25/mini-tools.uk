Open QR Generator

What this tool does

The QR Code Generator creates scannable QR codes from links or short text. Use it for menus, signup forms, support pages, event pages, product labels, printed flyers and quick mobile testing.

How to use it

  1. Open the QR Generator and enter the URL or text you want to share.
  2. Generate the QR code and check that it is easy to scan.
  3. Download or copy the QR image if the tool provides that option.
  4. Test the QR code with a phone before printing or publishing it.

Best use cases

  • Create a QR code for a website, form, payment page or menu.
  • Add a QR code to flyers, packaging, classroom materials or posters.
  • Share support links or onboarding pages without typing long URLs.
  • Generate a quick QR code for testing mobile flows.

FAQ

What can I put in a QR code?
Most short text or URLs work well. Very long content can make a QR code harder to scan.
