What this tool does

The UK VAT Calculator helps you add VAT to a net amount or remove VAT from a gross amount. It is useful for checking invoices, quotes, receipts, marketplace listings and quick business price estimates.

How to use it

  1. Open the VAT Calculator and enter the price or amount.
  2. Choose the VAT rate, such as the standard UK rate or a custom rate.
  3. Select add VAT or remove VAT depending on your calculation.
  4. Review the net amount, VAT amount and gross total.

Best use cases

  • Calculate 20% UK VAT on a net price before sending a quote.
  • Remove VAT from a gross receipt to find the net amount.
  • Compare standard, reduced and custom VAT rates for quick estimates.
  • Check invoice line items without opening a spreadsheet.

FAQ

Can I remove VAT from a total?
Yes. Enter the gross amount and choose remove VAT to estimate the net amount and VAT amount.
