Estimate PAYE income tax, National Insurance and monthly take-home pay when comparing jobs, planning a raise or checking a UK payslip.

Open UK Tax Calculator

What this tool does

The UK Salary After Tax Calculator estimates how much of a gross annual salary may remain after PAYE income tax and National Insurance. It is for planning job offers, salary changes and monthly budgets.

How to use it

  1. Open the calculator and enter your annual gross salary in pounds.
  2. Select the available UK tax year option.
  3. Calculate the estimate to see tax, National Insurance and take-home pay.
  4. Use the result as a planning estimate, not as tax or financial advice.

Best use cases

  • Estimate monthly take-home pay before accepting a UK job offer.
  • Compare salary increases, bonus scenarios and gross-to-net income.
  • Check how PAYE and National Insurance affect your annual salary.
  • Plan personal budgets with a quick UK salary calculator.

FAQ

Is this tax advice?
No. It is an estimate for planning. Confirm complex situations with HMRC or a qualified adviser.
