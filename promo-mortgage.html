Estimate monthly mortgage payments, total repayment, interest cost and loan-to-value ratio before comparing UK mortgage options.

Open Mortgage Calculator

What this tool does

The UK Mortgage Calculator estimates monthly repayments, total interest and loan-to-value ratio from property value, loan amount, term and interest rate. It helps UK users compare possible mortgage scenarios before speaking with a lender.

How to use it

  1. Enter the property value and the mortgage loan amount.
  2. Add the mortgage term in years and the expected interest rate.
  3. Choose repayment or interest-only mode.
  4. Review the estimated monthly payment, total repayment, interest and LTV.

Best use cases

  • Estimate monthly repayments for a first home purchase.
  • Compare repayment and interest-only mortgage scenarios.
  • Check LTV before speaking with a lender or broker.
  • Plan affordability when interest rates or term length change.

FAQ

Is this a mortgage offer?
No. It is an estimate for planning. Actual rates depend on lender checks and personal circumstances.
